What is Merit?

Merit-style teaching involves employing a proficient facilitator or instructor to prompt student-student engagement through the presentation of demanding problem sets or similar activities. The facilitator offers guidance as students collaborate in small groups, fostering interaction and discussion of each student's approach within the group.
