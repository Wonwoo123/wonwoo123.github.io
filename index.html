Overview

Welcome! I am a researcher specializing in algebraic combinatorics, topological data analysis (TDA), and quantitative finance. My work often involves the study of cluster algebras, snake graphs, and the application of persistent Laplacians to machine learning models.

Currently, I am exploring how mathematical structures like dimer covers and skein relations can be unified with computational methods to solve complex problems in both theoretical math and financial market prediction.
