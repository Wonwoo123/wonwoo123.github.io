About Me

Welcome! I am a postdoc researcher in mathematics. I'm currently working at ICMS-Sofia. My research interests include cluster algebras, topological data analysis (TDA), and quantitative finance.

I am a postdoctoral researcher in International Center for Mathematical Sciences – Sofia (ICMS-Sofia). I did my Ph.D at University of Illinois, Urbana Champaign. My advisor was Professor Rinat Kedem.
